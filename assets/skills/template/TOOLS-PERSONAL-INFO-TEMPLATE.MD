### Remediation Priority

- **High Risk (Must Remediate Immediately)**:
  - Collecting personal sensitive information from illegal sources or beyond the minimum necessary scope;
  - Personal information stored/transmitted in plaintext (no encryption, no secure channel);
  - Processing or cross-border provision of personal information without obtaining consent/separate consent;
  - No remediation or notification mechanism after personal information leakage.
- **Medium Risk (Recommended to Remediate Soon)**:
  - Unreasonable retention period settings, no deletion/anonymization mechanism;
  - No Personal Information Protection Impact Assessment (PIA) conducted for high-risk processing;
  - Lack of transparency/refusal mechanism for automated decision-making.
- **Low Risk (Recommended for Improvement)**:
  - Incomplete anonymization, redundant retention of personal information in logs;
  - Incomplete or unclear privacy policy/user agreement disclosures.
